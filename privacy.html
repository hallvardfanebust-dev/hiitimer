Privacy Policy

HIITimer for iPhone · Last updated 2 August 2026

Norsk (bokmål) · Support

In short: HIITimer has no user accounts and no sign-in. Your workout settings stay on your iPhone and are never sent to us. We do not run any analytics. The free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob, and Google processes some device data to deliver them — that is the only third party involved in your use of the app, apart from Apple's own handling of purchases.

1. Who is responsible

HIITimer is developed and published by Hallvard Fanebust, an individual developer based in Norway. For the purposes of the EU/EEA General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Hallvard Fanebust is the data controller for the limited processing described below.

Contact: hallvard.fanebust@gmail.com

2. Data stored on your device

HIITimer stores the following on your iPhone only. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else:

  • Your saved workout presets (name, work time, rest time, number of intervals and sets).
  • The configuration you last started a workout with, so the home screen widget can repeat it.
  • Your chosen audio cue level.
  • A simple count of completed workouts, used solely to decide when to ask you once whether you would like to rate the app.

This information is held inside the app's own storage area. It is removed when you delete the app. It may be included in a device backup that you yourself make to iCloud or to a computer; such a backup is controlled by you and by Apple, not by us.

HIITimer does not read your health data, your location, your contacts, your photos, your microphone or your camera. It requests no such permissions.

3. Purchases

HIITimer Pro can be bought as a monthly subscription, an annual subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. All payment handling is performed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment card details, your billing address, or your Apple Account.

The app asks Apple whether the current device is entitled to Pro features, and Apple answers yes or no. Apple additionally provides us with aggregated, anonymised sales and subscription reports which cannot be traced back to an individual buyer.

Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising (free version only)

The free version of HIITimer displays a banner on the configuration screen and an interstitial ad after a completed workout. These ads are delivered by Google AdMob. If you buy HIITimer Pro, no ads are shown.

To deliver, cap, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google may process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), if you have permitted its use;
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it (typically at city or region level);
  • Device and operating system information, such as model, iOS version and language;
  • Interactions with the ads themselves, such as whether an ad was shown or tapped.

Google acts as an independent controller for this processing. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Consent and tracking choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, HIITimer shows you a consent dialogue before any personalised advertising takes place, using Google's certified consent management platform. You can decline. You can also reopen the dialogue at any time from the app's settings and change your answer.

Separately, iOS may ask for your permission before an app is allowed to use the advertising identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites. Declining costs you nothing: you will still be able to use every part of the free version, and you will still see ads — they will simply be less relevant.

You can review or reset your advertising identifier at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising on your iPhone.

6. What we do not do

  • We operate no servers and no database. The app works entirely offline apart from loading ads.
  • We use no analytics or crash-reporting service, and we build no profile of you.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal data, because we hold none.
  • We send no marketing email; there is nowhere in the app to give us an email address.

7. Legal basis for processing

Where GDPR applies, the legal basis for personalised advertising is your consent (Article 6(1)(a)), which you may withdraw at any time. Non-personalised advertising and basic ad delivery rest on our legitimate interest in funding a free app (Article 6(1)(f)). Processing connected to a purchase is necessary to perform the contract you enter into (Article 6(1)(b)).

8. Retention

We retain no personal data ourselves, so there is nothing on our side to delete. Data held on your device is erased when you delete the app. Retention periods for advertising data are set by Google and described in Google's own policy; retention of purchase records is set by Apple and by the accounting law we are subject to.

9. Children

HIITimer is a general-audience fitness tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. Because we hold no personal data about you, requests concerning advertising data should be addressed to Google, and requests concerning purchases to Apple. We will help you direct a request if you are unsure — write to the address above.

If you believe your data has been handled unlawfully you may complain to your national supervisory authority. In Norway this is Datatilsynet, datatilsynet.no.

11. International transfers

Google and Apple are global companies and may process data outside the EEA, including in the United States. Both rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework for such transfers. We initiate no transfers of our own.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of the page will be updated and the change described here. Continued use of the app after a change means the updated policy applies.
